MISSION
To strengthen and nurture our most valued relationships by eliminating the communication gaps and misunderstandings that cause relational friction.
VISION
A world where every person is supported by a secure, deeply trustworthy core circle of five close relationships.
The Inspiration — Genesis

Growing up, I was surrounded by a wide, deeply connected circle of close friends. But as the years passed, I watched that number quietly and unknowingly dwindle. I realized that people rarely make a conscious choice to walk away; instead, they slowly drift apart through the silent friction and accumulated misunderstandings of modern digital communication.

The Inspiration — The Shift

I reached a point where I refused to let my remaining core circle shrink any further. SarthAI was born from a deeply personal need to intercept that relational decay. By bridging the 'Context Gap' and translating true intent, it is built to protect the connections we value most, ensuring that a simple misunderstanding never costs us a meaningful relationship again.
